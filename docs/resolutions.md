# Park resolutions (#11)

The park can be drawn wider than the game's 640x480: any width from 640 to
1920 (in steps of 8), 480 high. The panel, the bar of icons along the bottom,
stays 640 wide at the left, as do the game's own full screens (finance,
research and the rest), which draw 640 wide with black to their right (the finance
and other windows drawn over the park keep the park beside them).

| How | |
|---|---|
| F10 (Windows) | *Park resolution*: 640x480, 800x480, 856x480 (16:9), 1120x480 (21:9), or *Fit the screen* (the monitor's shape at 480 high) |
| F10 (Linux) | *More*, then *Park resolution*, with the same choices |
| command line | `--res 856x480` |
| themepark.ini | `[game] res=856x480` (the menu writes it) |

A change takes effect the next time the game sets 640x480 up: when a park
opens, or on R (twice, from 640x480). In a window, the window takes the new
shape at the same scale; any scaling but integer keeps the pixels square.

## How it works

The game has one 640x480 mode set (0x46ECF0) and keeps the screen's width and
height in 0x4CE5B8 and 0x4CE5C0, but most of its code has 640 and 480 as
constants. The work is in three parts.

**The mode.** The VESA call under the mode set (0x46E658) asks for mode
0x17F instead of 0x101. dos32 offers 0x17F at a size the host sets with
`dos32_vesa_custom(w, h)` (pcrecomp branch `dos32-custom-vesa`). `hires_mode`
(game.c) sets `tp_w`/`tp_h` before the mode set runs, since it sets the mouse
range, then writes the screen size and the clip window (0x4CE5C8–0x4CE5F0).
The 320x200 mode set (0x46EC50) puts 640x480 back, because much of the
patched code is shared by both modes. 320x200 is drawn in 640x400 units:
0x4CE5F6 is 1 in 320x200 and 8 in 640x480.

**The buffers.** The park is drawn in two back buffers that the game loads as
one resource, `*WB_SCREEN` (0x4A10FA). Its size word is 0x4A1140, 0x96000 in
the game. `resource_load` sizes it for 1920x(480+16) twice, about 1.9 MB, at
start-up, so a size picked later in the menu fits.

**The constants.** `run_lift.py` lifts chosen instructions with a variable in
place of their constant (`SCREEN_SITES`). The table is generated by
`tools/screen_sites.py` from the lifted C's instruction comments:

| What | Sites | Becomes |
|---|---|---|
| 640: row pitches, clips, right edges, the view's width in tiles, the mouse range, the top bar | 84 | `tp_w` |
| 480: heights, clips, rows of the full-screen copy | 111 | `tp_h` (always 480 for now) |
| 638 / 478: the pointer's clamps, the mouse range's far corner | 43 | `tp_w - 2` / `tp_h - 2` |
| 640·k: the unrolled 16-row copy (0x445C24), the 30-strip loop (0x444F06, 0x444EF5), the panel clear (0x445FB6, and its 400 at 0x445FAE) | 31 + 1 | `k * tp_w` |
| 0xA0 dwords per row: the screen copy and fill (0x471CC2, 0x4715C5) | 2 | `tp_w / 4` |
| the fonts' step back to the next character (0x470F23, 0x470FC9) | 2 | `4 - 3·tp_w`, `4 - 6·tp_w` |

The following are left alone:
- 640s that centre text in a 640-wide box (the panel's text, through
  0x42F377).
- The dirty grid's clip (0x444D85–0x444DF0).
- Stack and structure offsets.

Four more changes, each an override in game.c:

- **Presenting.** The game shows a full screen as five unrolled VESA bank
  copies: four of 64 KB and one of 0xB000 bytes, nine places in all. At the
  fifth, a host call (`tp_present_tail`, `tp_clear_tail`) copies or clears
  the rest of `tp_w x tp_h`. It switches banks the way the game's switch
  (0x46E6BC) does, but without that function's check, which turns colour 0
  red past bank 5. The game's other calls to it get the same check widened
  (`SCREEN_SITES` 0x46E6D1): bank 15 at any width but 640.
- **The dirty grid.** The screen update (0x444ED6) copies the 32x16 cells its
  grid marks, and the grid covers 640x480 only. Wider, the update takes its
  whole-screen branch every frame.
- **The game's own screens.** The stock market, research, the bank and 15
  more are 320x200 pictures that 0x445EFA doubles into the back buffer, rows
  of 640 one after another. `double_screen` doubles them at the screen's
  pitch.
- **The mouse zones.** The park's zones are 12 bytes each at 0x4A93C3.
  0x43DFAC sets their heights for the mode. `park_zones` sets the widths of
  the edge-scroll strips, the view and the message bar to the screen's
  width; the panel and the buttons at the top right stay where they are.

At 640x480 every variable holds the game's own value, and conformance is 6/6.

## Not done

- **Heights other than 480.** The panel's place, the view's height and the
  mouse's y range are fixed by many constants, so 800x600 draws a 480-high
  layout with a blank band below it. The 480 sites already read `tp_h`; the
  400s and the panel's y coordinates are next.
- **The game's own screens.** They stay 640 wide at the left. Their
  background fill reaches a little past 640.
- **Hand play.** Only the headless checks below, and one short windowed run,
  have been done.

## Tested

Headless, Windows (and 856x480 on Linux):
- At 800x480, 856x480 and 1120x480, the park fills the width, and the panel
  and the date sit where they do at 640.
- Clicks at the far right pick the right tile: x=760 gives column 28 at
  24 pixels a tile.
- Scrolling reaches the map's right edge.
- R twice (to 320x200 and back) keeps the size.
- The finance screen opens over the wide park, with the same figures as at
  640.
- Conformance is 6/6 at the default.
